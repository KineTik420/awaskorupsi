Corruption Awareness

What is Corruption?

Corruption refers to dishonest or illegal behaviour by people in positions of power. It includes bribery, abuse of authority, fraud, and embezzlement.

Effects on Society

Corruption weakens public trust, stunts economic growth, diverts public funds from essential services, and reduces fairness and equality in society.

How to Prevent It

  • Promote transparency in government
  • Encourage ethical leadership
  • Report suspicious activities
  • Educate the public and youth
  • Support independent oversight bodies

Your Rights

Citizens have the right to access public information, challenge corrupt decisions, and report wrongdoing without fear of retaliation.
